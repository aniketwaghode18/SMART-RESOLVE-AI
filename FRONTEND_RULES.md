1. Design Rules

I do not want my website to look like a generic AI-generated or "vibe-coded" product.

Never use purple gradients.
Never use pill-shaped buttons.
Never use fake reviews, testimonials, ratings, or customer feedback.
Never use fake metrics, fabricated statistics, or fake customer counters.
Never use vague, generic, or meaningless hero text.
Never use emoji as interface icons.
Never use em dashes in website copy.
Never use over-the-top scroll animations.
Never use cursor animations, cursor-following effects, or custom cursor trails.
Never use AI-slop photos, generic AI-generated stock-looking imagery, or irrelevant decorative images.
Never use AI-slop copy, generic marketing filler, or repetitive promotional text.
Never fabricate customer logos, awards, certifications, or social proof.
Never present invented data or capabilities as real.
Never add unnecessary decorative effects that make the interface look generic or artificial.

Create a professional, intentional, distinctive UI appropriate to the actual product. Do not sacrifice authenticity for visual trends.

2. Launch Requirements

The website must not be considered ready for launch until every requirement below has been completed and verified.

Connect the custom domain.
Verify that the custom domain works correctly.
Add a favicon and verify that it loads correctly.
Remove the unwanted "Made with AI" tag and website-builder branding.
Create and publish a privacy policy page.
Create and publish a terms and conditions page.
Verify that both legal pages are accessible through the appropriate website links.

Do not claim launch readiness while any mandatory requirement remains incomplete or unverified.

Do not fabricate legal information or claim legal compliance without verification.

3. Accuracy and Verification
Do not invent facts, metrics, reviews, testimonials, or product capabilities.
Do not claim that a feature works unless it has been implemented and verified.
Do not claim that a test has passed unless it has actually been run and passed.
Do not claim that the domain, favicon, or legal pages are configured correctly without verifying them.
Inspect the actual website and fix identified mistakes before declaring the work complete.
If something cannot be verified, explicitly state that it remains unverified.
Never mark an incomplete requirement as complete.
Do not ignore these rules when creating, modifying, or reviewing frontend UI.

These rules are mandatory for all frontend design, website copy, visual assets, animations, and launch preparation.
