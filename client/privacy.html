Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026. Last updated: October 16, 2026.

1. Information We Collect

SmartResolve AI collects information necessary to provide e-commerce customer support, process inquiries, and coordinate ticket escalations:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, password hashes, and membership level.
  • Order Information: Order identifiers, product descriptions, transaction amounts, delivery dates, and tracking status.
  • Communication Records: Customer messages, agent responses, conversation timestamps, and AI analysis records (including intent classifications and resolution metadata).

2. How We Use Information

We process collected data strictly to operate our intelligent support platform:

  • Classifying customer inquiries to match applicable store policies and order records.
  • Drafting policy-grounded resolution proposals and answering support questions.
  • Escalating complex, high-urgency, or ambiguous inquiries to human support agents.
  • Auditing system accuracy and tracking prompt performance benchmarks.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement technical and organizational controls to protect your data:

  • Cryptographic hashing of passwords using bcrypt with salt rounds.
  • Stateless token authentication using signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • Strict redaction of sensitive credentials and secrets in server logs.
  • Input sanitization and safety guardrails to intercept prompt injection attempts.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Inquiry text is submitted to certified Large Language Model providers (Google Cloud Gemini API or OpenAI API) solely for real-time inference. Your data is not sold or distributed to third-party data brokers.

5. Customer Rights

Customers have the right to inspect their order history, view active and past support conversations, and request account termination. Inquiries may be directed through our customer support interface.

6. Contact Us

For questions or privacy concerns regarding SmartResolve AI, contact support at privacy@smartresolve.local.
